The winds

Why the route changes every morning

Three winds make the weather on this coast. The captain reads them at dawn, then proposes the anchorage of the day.

  1. North‑west

    The mistral

    It sweeps down the Rhône valley, strong and dry, and leaves behind a sky of rare clarity. The captain then looks for anchorages east of the capes: Pampelonne, the coves of the Estérel, Villefranche.

  2. East and south‑east

    The easterly

    More humid, it raises a long swell. You tuck in west of the capes and into the deep harbours, or stay in port for a morning to explore the village.

  3. In the afternoon

    The sea breeze

    On fine days, the land warms up and the sea blows towards the coast after lunch. It is the hour of the sailing yachts, and the moment to move to the night's anchorage.

The seasons

When to cruise, when to buy

  1. 01

    May and June

    Water at 18 to 22 °C

    The coast awakens. The anchorages are free, the light lingers, and charter rates are those of the low season.

  2. 02

    July and August

    Water at 24 to 26 °C

    High season. The best yachts are booked in winter and the ports are full: a broker who knows the captains makes all the difference.

  3. 03

    September and October

    Water at 20 to 23 °C

    Our favourite season. The sea is still warm, the crowds have gone, and Monaco hosts the Yacht Show.

  4. 04

    November to April

    Winter lay‑up

    The yachts head to the yards. It is the best time to buy: viewings in peace, surveys out of the water, negotiations without the pressure of summer.

Anchoring without harm

The Posidonia seagrass meadows

These underwater meadows shelter fish, clear the water and hold the sand on the beaches. A yacht's anchor can tear out square metres of them, and they grow back by barely a few centimetres a year.

Since 2019, vessels over twenty‑four metres may no longer anchor in the seagrass meadows of French Mediterranean waters. Our captains know the sandy areas and the authorised buoys: you enjoy the cove, and it stays intact.
