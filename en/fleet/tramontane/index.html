About Tramontane

Tramontane is the liveliest of the fleet: she runs from Saint‑Tropez to Cannes in an hour, leaving time for lunch at the Lérins islands before heading home. Her grey hull and tinted glazing give her a sporting look.

On board, grey eucalyptus, black leather and brushed steel make for a disciplined, almost architectural interior. The flybridge, sheltered by a hardtop, becomes the main lounge as soon as the sun is high.
