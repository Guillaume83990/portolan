About Alizé

Alizé was designed for a family that spends a lot of time at sea: bright volumes, a large foredeck terrace, and a hull that glides along at twenty‑four knots in near silence. She runs with a crew of five, which keeps her operating costs reasonable.

Bleached oak, travertine, rattan and olive and terracotta fabrics give her the feel of a holiday home. She is moored in the old port of Saint‑Tropez and can be viewed by appointment.
