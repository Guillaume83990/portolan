About Mistral Blanc

Mistral Blanc was delivered in Livorno and fully refreshed in 2024: white lacquer, pale ash, ivory leather and touches of soft gold. Everything aboard is filled with light, right up to the grand saloon on the main deck.

Based in Monaco, she happily heads for Portofino or Corsica. Her chef spent ten years in Michelin‑starred houses on the Riviera; the table is laid on the aft deck, facing the harbour as it lights up.
