About Camarat

Camarat was designed for the Mediterranean summer: tall floor‑to‑ceiling windows, a saloon that flows onto the aft deck without a threshold, and an owner's suite set at the bow, facing the sea. Inside, walnut answers travertine and bottle‑green velvet, always under indirect light.

Her crew of nine has known her since her launch. At anchor off Pampelonne, her stabilisers keep her perfectly still; underway, she links Saint‑Tropez to Monaco in under five hours.
