About Castellane

Castellane belongs to that family of Dutch yachts that move fast without ever making themselves heard. Her navy aluminium hull runs at seventeen knots and reaches Corsica within the day.

On board, light oak and sand‑coloured linen soften the taut lines of her exterior. The beach club, at water level, opens on three sides when she lies at anchor off the Lérins islands, her home waters.
